## Key decisions made

- **ADR-0033 accepted** — application-layer owner resolution over per-adapter
  stamping: one resolution point instead of O(N adapters), IDOR-by-construction
  impossible, verified by a fitness rule.
- **Empty query records deleted** rather than kept as envelopes (YAGNI);
  reintroducing a record later is mechanical.
- **DDL**: hex-color and display-order CHECKs as defense in depth; no sibling
  name uniqueness (would be a DB-only constraint without a domain invariant);
  `parent_id` indexed; no ON DELETE action (categories archive, never delete).

## Concepts explained / learned

- **Recursive CTE for ancestor walks**: `WITH RECURSIVE` seed/recursive-member
  structure; `UNION` deduplicates and therefore terminates on (corrupt) cycles
  where `UNION ALL` would loop; the owner filter belongs in both members
  because the self-FK cannot enforce same-owner parenting.
- **The refactor's fail-closed property**, demonstrated involuntarily: the one
  service the author missed (`GetCategoryService`) failed to *compile* —
  before ADR-0033, forgetting the owner stamp in an adapter compiled silently.
